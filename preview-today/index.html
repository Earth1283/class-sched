Settings New

Preferences → Today's schedule

A small widget with today's classes that stays on the desktop.
It never covers your work. Other windows sit on top of it.
Closing the main window leaves the widget running in the background.
How A would work

Position: drag it anywhere; it remembers where you left it, and which screen.

No taskbar button, and it stays out of Alt+Tab.

Live: the “now” row and progress bar update every 30 seconds, and it rolls over at midnight.

Limits: it lives inside the app, so quitting the app removes it unless “Keep it after I close the app” is on. Win+D (Show desktop) may hide it on some Windows versions.

How B would work

A timeline strip along the bottom of the main window: today's classes laid out by time, with a red “now” line.

The ✕ hides it, and the setting brings it back. The week grid above simply gets a little shorter.

It only shows while the main window is open, and it can't sit behind other apps.
