Motion

  • Menus open from the cursor: 160 ms, items stagger in
  • Classes ease up on hover, press down on click
  • Week change slides in the direction you moved, classes stagger in
  • Course checkboxes pop and draw the tick
  • The now-line dot pulses softly every 3 s
  • Delete and hide fade out, with an Undo toast that springs up
  • Create button lifts on hover, presses on click

Everything stays under 350 ms, and it all switches off when Windows' “Show animations” is off.
